## The build order research converged on

From `.planning/research/SUMMARY.md`, reconciled across all four documents:

1. Storage seam + test harness (`internal/store`) — nothing downstream is buildable or safely testable without it
2. Local write queue (`internal/queue`) — generalizes phase 01's mechanism; blocks every conversion

6. Backup, restore, restore drill — before/alongside the graph, since AGE has its own restore hazards
7. Apache AGE graph

## Two research findings that must not be banked as settled

Both are MEDIUM confidence and flagged for re-verification at the point they shape a phase:

- **`pg_upgrade` does not support databases with AGE installed** (OID-referencing types), so every Postgres major upgrade is dump/restore — and a `pg_dump`/`pg_restore` can *succeed* while leaving the graph unqueryable unless the drill runs a live Cypher check. Partly vendor-doc-derived. Re-verify against `github.com/apache/age`.
- **AGE's Cypher coverage** is a dated snapshot. Re-check against the pinned version.

Stack's version facts are the opposite — HIGH confidence, verified against the
GitHub Releases/Tags API on 2026-09-11. Do not re-research those.
